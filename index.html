Before & After Photo Slider

Two aligned photos, one drag handle. Pair two images with the same name convention — before.jpg and after.jpg — in a folder, list the folder in sets.js, and this page shows the comparison. Static files only: no backend, no uploads, no analytics, no metrics.

Use the keyboard: focus a handle and press ArrowLeft/ArrowRight to move it, Home/End to jump to fully after/before.
